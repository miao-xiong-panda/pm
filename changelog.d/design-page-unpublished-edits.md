A design page that a draft changes now says, at its top, how many unpublished edits it has and which sprint's close
publishes them.

### Added

- On the site, the version bar of a published design page that a records workspace or the store's edits change
  opens with one line per draft: "N unpublished edits in draft D, published when sprint S (title) closes", N being
  the sections its diff marks changed, added or removed, with links to view the draft and to see what changed, the
  diff that highlights each changed line. A draft no open sprint links says that no sprint close publishes it yet.
